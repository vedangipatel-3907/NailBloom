Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

How do I book an appointment?

Open Book, choose your service, artist, date and time, then confirm.

Can I cancel my appointment?

Yes. Open My Appointments and click Cancel on a confirmed booking.

Is online payment required?

No. This demo project does not process real payments.

Are the images real salon work?

The project uses publicly hosted demo photography for visual presentation.

Does the project need a database?

No. Booking and preference data are stored with browser LocalStorage.
